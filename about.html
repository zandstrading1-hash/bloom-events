About Bloom Events

We’re an event rental and styling business based in Macomb, Michigan, serving weddings, showers and parties across Metro Detroit.

Let’s start with your space.

Maybe you know exactly which flower wall you want. Maybe you have a color in mind and need a little help with the rest. Either way, send us your date and a few details about your venue.

Our rentals include flower walls, greenery backdrops, white pedestals, bloom bars and sweets carts. We’ll talk through what fits your event and arrange the setup with you.
