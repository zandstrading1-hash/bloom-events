About this website

The Bloom Events website presents rental options and provides ways to ask about an event. Browsing a page, sending a message or receiving an automated acknowledgment does not itself book a date, hold inventory or create a rental agreement.

For a rental, the agreed services, dates, price and responsibilities must be set out in your written quote and rental agreement. These website terms do not change an agreement you already have.

Prices, availability and photographs

Starting prices help you plan. The final quote depends on the selected items and agreed services. Ask for the total, including applicable taxes and any delivery, setup or other charges, before agreeing to a booking.

Photographs show the collection or a labeled reference. Screen colors, lighting and event styling can affect how an item looks. Flowers, cakes, signage and other accessories shown in a photograph are included only when listed in your quote. Images identified as retouched references have been edited.

Availability and specifications should be confirmed for your event. Corrections to website information do not by themselves authorize new charges or change an accepted agreement.

Website content and reference images

Website text, branding and photographs may be protected by intellectual-property rights belonging to Bloom Events or the relevant rights holder. You may browse and share page links for planning your event. Ask for permission before reusing content commercially, except where applicable law permits that use.

When you send a reference photo, only send material you are entitled to share for planning. Sending a reference or an inquiry does not grant permission to use your name, guest photographs or event images in advertising.

Respectful use

Please provide accurate contact and event details. Do not use the website to impersonate someone, send unlawful or abusive content, attempt unauthorized access, or interfere with other visitors' use of the site.

Privacy and external services

The Privacy policy explains the handling of inquiry information and website data. Following an external link or opening your email or messaging app may take you to a service with separate terms and privacy practices.

Your rights and questions

Nothing on this page excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded, including applicable consumer-protection rights. It does not require arbitration, waive a right to bring a claim, or limit lawful consumer reviews.

If information appears incorrect or you have a concern, contact hello@bloomevents.com or (586) 360-4200. For event changes, also review the Booking & cancellation policy.
