Confirming a reservation

Start by sending your event date, venue and the rentals you have in mind. We will discuss availability and the proposed setup. An inquiry alone does not reserve an item or date.

Your written quote and rental agreement should identify the items, event date, rental period, total price, services included, and any steps needed to confirm the reservation. Ask for written confirmation before treating a booking as secured.

Deposits and payments

The amount of any deposit, its refund conditions, payment dates and accepted payment methods must be confirmed in your written agreement before you pay. This page does not set a standard deposit percentage or payment schedule.

Ask for an itemized total showing applicable taxes and any delivery, setup, collection, custom-work or other charges. Do not send card details through a general inquiry.

Cancellations and refunds

If you need to cancel, contact us as soon as possible with the booking name and event date. Use the notice method and deadline stated in your agreement, keep a copy, and request acknowledgment.

Any refund or cancellation charge depends on your agreement and applicable law. This page does not make all deposits non-refundable, promise an automatic full refund, or impose a cancellation fee. If your agreement does not clearly explain the result of cancellation, ask us to clarify it before booking.

If Bloom cannot provide an agreed service, contact us to discuss the situation and the remedies available under your agreement and applicable law. Your statutory rights are not limited by this page.

Date changes and rescheduling

Contact us before changing the date, venue or rental items. A new date or revised setup is subject to availability. Any price difference, transfer of a payment or other change should be confirmed in writing before you agree to it.

Venue access, delivery and collection

Please share venue access times, loading restrictions, stairs or elevators, required insurance documents and setup rules before booking. Delivery, setup and collection arrangements belong in the written agreement.

For outdoor events, discuss a safe placement and weather plan in advance. If conditions become unsafe, keep guests clear of the equipment and contact us. Weather-related changes and any resulting costs should be addressed in your agreement.

Care of rental items

Use the pieces only as agreed. Do not climb on, move, attach items to or alter a display without guidance. Keep children supervised around rental equipment and report damage or a safety concern promptly.

Responsibility for loss, damage, cleaning or late collection, and how any charge is calculated, must be set out in your agreement. This page does not impose automatic replacement charges or a new damage waiver.

Contact about an existing booking

Email hello@bloomevents.com or call (586) 360-4200. Include your booking name and event date. For formal notices, follow the contact method specified in your agreement.
