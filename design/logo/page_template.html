My recommendation

H if she wants heads to turn, A if she wants timeless

The Window is the coolest of the eight and the hardest to copy, because it’s made of Bloom’s own flowers. It will stop people scrolling, and the version-per-wall idea gives every booking its own matching logo. The Backdrop is the classic choice: it will still look right in ten years and works on everything.

  • For fun and parties, F (Groovy) is the one. For weddings, B (The Rings).
  • G (Paper Bloom) is the best if the website or Instagram reels matter most, because it moves.
  • Mixing is fine. For example, the Window letters for social and the website, with the Paper Bloom rose as the profile picture.
