Frequently Asked

Common questions

Everything you might be wondering about pricing, booking, setup, and the day of your event. Still curious about something? Reach out.

Booking & Pricing

What if I need to cancel or change my date?+

Contact us as soon as possible and follow the notice instructions in your rental agreement. Any refund, cancellation charge or date transfer depends on that agreement and applicable law. Read the Booking & cancellation policy.

How far in advance should I book? +

Contact us when you have a date and venue in mind. Availability depends on the pieces and services you need. If your event is soon, ask about availability and any additional charges before booking.

Do you require a deposit? +

Any deposit amount, refund conditions and balance deadline will be stated in your written rental agreement before you pay. Ask us to confirm these details with your quote. See our Booking & cancellation policy.

How much does a typical event cost? +

Most of our clients spend between $400 and $2,500 depending on the setup. À la carte pieces start at $275 (pillar settings), and our most popular bundle — the Bridal Suite (flower wall + bloom bar) — runs $720.

Every event is custom-quoted based on your size, palette, and venue. Quotes are free.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Ask about payment options when requesting a quote. Any installment schedule, accepted payment method and fee must be confirmed in your written agreement.

Setup & Logistics

Do you handle setup and breakdown? +

Yes. We handle delivery, setup and breakdown.

Setup typically happens 1–2 hours before your event start time. Breakdown happens immediately after, so we're not in the way.

How long does setup take? +

Most setups take 60 to 90 minutes. A full bloom (all four signature pieces) takes about 2 hours. We arrive with everything pre-built so on-site setup is fast and quiet.

Do you travel outside Macomb? +

We serve Metro Detroit and surrounding areas. Share your venue address so we can confirm service availability and any delivery or travel charges in your quote.

What if my venue has restrictions? +

Share your venue’s installation rules, access times and any insurance requirements before booking. We’ll confirm whether the selected pieces and setup are suitable.

Customization

Can you match my color palette? +

Yes — every setup is built to your palette. During the planning phase we'll ask for your colors (Pinterest boards welcome) and pull samples for approval before your event.

Can I request a custom design? +

Always. If you've seen something on Pinterest, Instagram, or a wedding magazine, send it our way. Reference photos help us understand what you have in mind.

Do you provide mood boards or renders? +

For Full Bloom packages and custom designs, yes — we provide a styled mood board with palette, florals, and reference images so you can see the vision before the event.

Day Of

Will you be on-site during the event? +

We're on-site for setup and breakdown, but not during the event itself (unless you've booked an attendant for your bloom bar). The setup is designed to stay in place throughout your event.

What if something goes wrong? +

Keep guests clear of an affected display and contact us using the details in your rental agreement. Do not move or repair rental equipment without guidance. For an immediate danger or emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service.

Can guests take photos with everything? +

Guests are welcome to take photos with the setups. Taking or sharing a photo does not automatically give Bloom Events permission to use identifiable guest photos in advertising.
