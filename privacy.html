Who this notice is for

This notice describes the Bloom Events website and event inquiries made through the contact options shown here. Bloom Events is an event rental and styling business based in Macomb, Michigan.

You can browse the collection without creating an account. This website does not take online payments or confirm reservations automatically.

Information you share

When you contact us, you may share your name, email address, phone number, event date, venue, event type, rental preferences, budget and message. We also receive any reference images or other details you choose to send.

Only send information needed to discuss your event. Please do not send payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers, identification documents or sensitive information about your guests through an inquiry.

Website hosting and security services may receive technical details such as an IP address, browser type, requested page and time of access when your browser requests a page.

Why information is used

Event inquiry information is used to answer questions, discuss availability, prepare quotes, coordinate your requested services and keep related business records. Technical information may be used to deliver pages, troubleshoot problems and protect the website.

Sending an inquiry is not a subscription to promotional email or automated marketing texts.

Service providers and other recipients

Information may be handled by providers used for website hosting, business email, telephone and messaging. Event details may be shared with a venue or service provider when needed to carry out the arrangements you request. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to address fraud or a legal claim.

If an online inquiry form is available, it uses Web3Forms to deliver submissions to the business. Web3Forms and its providers process submitted information for delivery and spam prevention and may process it outside the United States. See the Web3Forms privacy notice for its practices. Form delivery is not currently enabled on this website.

Clicking an email, telephone or text link opens the relevant application on your device. That application's provider has its own privacy practices. External websites you choose to visit have their own notices.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently set analytics or advertising cookies, run ad pixels, or store a visitor profile in your browser. The gallery, photographs and videos are served with the site rather than embedded from social networks.

The website does not track you across other websites. Its behavior does not change in response to a browser’s Do Not Track signal because that cross-site tracking is not enabled. Your browser can also manage cookies or block storage through its settings.

If tracking or additional third-party services are introduced, this notice and any necessary visitor choices will need to be updated before those services are used.

Records and security

Inquiry and booking records may need to be retained to respond to you, provide services, maintain accounting records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of record and the reason it is kept.

Please contact us about a particular record if you want to know how it is handled or request its deletion. No method of internet transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Questions and privacy requests

You may contact us to ask about your information, request a correction, or request access or deletion. Depending on where you live and which laws apply, additional rights may be available. We may need to verify a requester's identity before releasing or changing personal information and may need to keep certain records where the law permits or requires it.

Use hello@bloomevents.com or call (586) 360-4200. Please describe your request without sending sensitive documents in the first message.

Children’s information

The website is intended for adults planning events, including events for children. It is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information can contact us to request review and deletion where appropriate.

Updates to this notice

The date shown on this page will change when the notice is revised. If a change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law, that notice or consent will be provided before the relevant new use of information.
