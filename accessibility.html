If something is difficult to use

If a page, image, form or other part of this website is difficult for you to use, please tell us. We can discuss rental information and booking questions through another contact method.

Email hello@bloomevents.com, call (586) 360-4200, or send a text. Include the page or feature, what happened and your preferred way to receive a response. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis.

Features available on this site

  • A skip link to move past repeated navigation.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, gallery filters and photo-viewer controls.
  • Text descriptions for rental photographs and visible labels for form fields.
  • Layouts that adapt to smaller screens, with browser zoom available.
  • Video playback controls and reduced-motion support.

Ongoing review

Accessibility is part of maintaining the website. The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines provide a useful reference for reviewing barriers. The website has not received an independent accessibility certification, and some needs may require additional assistance.

Feedback helps identify issues that automated checks can miss. If your event is approaching, please mention the date when asking for help.
